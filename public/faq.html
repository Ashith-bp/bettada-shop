Questions and answers

About ordering, delivery, bulk supply and our products.

Ordering and payment

How do I pay?

Pay securely online with UPI, debit or credit card, or netbanking. Payment is handled by Cashfree, so your card details never reach our website.

Do I need an account?

You can create an account to save your addresses and see every order. Log in or sign up from the top of any page.

Can I cancel or return an order?

You can cancel before we dispatch. If your order arrives damaged or is not what you ordered, tell us within 48 hours. Read the refund and cancellation policy.

Where is my order?

Log in and open your account to see every order and its status. When your order ships, the tracking note appears there.

Delivery

How much is delivery?

Delivery is free on orders of ₹1,500 and above. Below that the charge depends on your state and the weight. You see the exact amount at checkout. See shipping and delivery.

How long does delivery take?

We dispatch within 2 to 4 working days of payment. Delivery then usually takes 3 to 7 working days, depending on where you live.

Bulk and export

What is the smallest bulk order?

Bulk orders start at 100 kg. Trade orders from 5 kg are welcome too. Send an enquiry and we will tell you what we can supply.

How do I get a price for a grade?

Prices for Arabica, Robusta and the pepper grades are quoted per order because they depend on grade, quantity and season. Use the bulk enquiry form, call us or message us on WhatsApp.

Do you supply anywhere in India?

Yes. Tell us your city and state in the enquiry and we confirm packing and delivery with your price.

Can I add a GST number?

Yes. There is an optional GST number field on the bulk enquiry form.

Our products

Is the coffee powder ground or whole bean?

The coffee powder is ground and ready for filter coffee, pour-over and moka pots. Ask us if you want whole beans.

What do the grade names mean?

See our grades for a short guide to Plantation A/B, Peaberry, Cherry, Kaapi Royale, MG1, TGSEB and FAQ.

How should I store coffee and pepper?

Keep both in an airtight container in a cool, dry place away from sunlight.

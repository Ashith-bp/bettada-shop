Our estate in Chikmagalur

Coffee and black pepper, grown, sun-dried and packed in Billenahalli, near KR Pete, Chikkamagalur.

Grown where coffee began in India

Coffee was first planted in India on the Baba Budan hills near Chikkamagalur, and the district is still the heart of Karnataka's coffee country. Our Arabica, Robusta and black pepper grow on the same land, and the pepper vines climb the shade trees above the coffee.

We cultivate shade-grown Arabica, bold Robusta and sun-dried Kalumenasu (black pepper) under dense silver oak canopies. We also partner directly with neighbouring Chikkamagaluru planters, so fresh harvests are available through the year.

From the plant to your door

  1. Picked ripeCoffee cherries and pepper berries are picked by hand when they are ready.
  2. Dried in the sunBoth crops are sun-dried before they are cleaned and graded.
  3. Graded and packedCoffee is graded or ground, pepper is cleaned, and both are packed on the estate.
  4. Sent to youPacks go out to your door. Bulk lots go to your city.

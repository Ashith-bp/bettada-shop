Shipping and delivery

Last updated 9 November 2026

We pack every order on our estate in Billenahalli, Chikkamagalur, Karnataka, and send it by courier to addresses across India.

Dispatch time

We dispatch your order within 2 to 4 working days after your payment is confirmed. Working days are Monday to Saturday, excluding public holidays. During busy periods it can take a little longer, and we will tell you if it does.

Delivery time

After dispatch, delivery usually takes 3 to 7 working days, depending on your location. Remote areas can take longer. Delivery times are estimates, because the courier controls the final leg.

Delivery charges

Delivery is free on orders with a product total of ₹1,500 or more. Below that, the charge depends on where you live and the weight of your order. You see the exact charge at checkout before you pay.

Tracking your order

When your order ships, we add the courier and tracking number to your order. Log in to see it. If you checked out as a guest, use the link on your order confirmation page.

Delivery address

Please check your address, pincode and mobile number before you pay, because the courier uses them to reach you. We cannot change the address after dispatch. If a parcel comes back because the address was wrong or nobody could be reached, we may ask you to pay for sending it again.

Bulk orders

Freight for bulk and trade orders is quoted separately. Send a bulk enquiry.

Outside India

We do not ship outside India through this website at the moment. Please contact us if you want to enquire about export.

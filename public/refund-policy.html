Refunds and cancellations

Last updated 9 November 2026

We want you to be happy with what you receive. This page explains what happens if you need to cancel an order or something goes wrong.

Cancelling an order

You can cancel an order before we dispatch it. Contact us on WhatsApp, by phone or by email with your order number, and we will cancel it and refund the full amount you paid. Once an order has been dispatched, it cannot be cancelled.

Returns

Coffee and pepper are food products, so for hygiene and safety we cannot accept returns of opened or used packs, or returns because you changed your mind.

Damaged or wrong items

If your order arrives damaged, leaking, or is not what you ordered, tell us within 48 hours of delivery. Send your order number and clear photos of the pack and the outer box. A short video of opening the parcel also helps. After we check, we will send a replacement or refund you, whichever you prefer where we can offer both.

Payment problems

If money left your account but your order did not go through, the bank or payment provider usually returns it automatically within 5 to 7 working days. If it does not, contact us with your transaction details and we will help.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the payment method you used. We start the refund within 3 working days after we approve it. It then takes about 5 to 7 working days to appear, depending on your bank or card issuer. Delivery charges are refunded when the whole order is cancelled before dispatch or when the problem was our mistake.

Bulk orders

Refund and cancellation terms for bulk and trade orders are agreed when we confirm the quote.

Privacy policy

Last updated 9 November 2026

Bettada Estate ("we", "us") runs this website. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We handle your information as required by the laws of India.

Information we collect

  • Account and order details: your name, email address, mobile number, delivery addresses and the items you order.
  • Your password, which we store only in a protected, scrambled form that we cannot read.
  • Enquiries: the details you send in our bulk enquiry form.
  • Payment information: payments are handled by Cashfree on its own secure page. We do not see or store your card, UPI or netbanking details. We receive only the result of the payment.

How we use it

  • To take, pack, deliver and support your orders.
  • To run your account, show your order history and keep your saved addresses.
  • To reply to your questions and enquiries.
  • To keep records that the law and our accounts require.

Who we share it with

  • Cashfree, our payment provider, to process your payment.
  • Courier companies, who need your name, mobile number and address to deliver your parcel.
  • Authorities, if the law requires us to.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not send it to advertisers.

Cookies and browser storage

When you log in, we set one cookie to keep you signed in. Your cart is kept in your browser so it is still there when you come back. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when a page loads, and the checkout page loads Cashfree's payment script.

How long we keep it

We keep your account until you ask us to delete it. We keep order records for as long as the law and our accounts require.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal information by contacting us. We will act on your request, except where we must keep records by law. You can also change your name, phone number and addresses yourself in your account.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, including encrypted connections and protected password storage. No website can promise perfect security, so please choose a strong password and keep it private.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.

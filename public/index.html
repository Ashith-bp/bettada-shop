Grown on our estate. Curated across Chikkamagaluru.

Pure, traceable harvests from the Western Ghats, never mixed with commercial fillers.

At our family plantation in Chikkamagaluru, we cultivate shade-grown Arabica, bold Robusta, and sun-dried Kalumenasu (black pepper) under dense silver oak canopies. To keep fresh harvests available year-round and to show the best micro-climates of the region, we also partner directly with neighbouring Chikkamagaluru planters. Every batch, whether from our own slopes or a partner estate, goes through moisture, bean-size and cupping checks before it is roasted or packed for your kitchen.
